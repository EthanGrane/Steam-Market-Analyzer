Privacy Policy
Steam Market Analyzer

Last updated: April 27, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Who We Are

Steam Market Analyzer is an independent app developed for indie game developers and market researchers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation.

Data We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect the following information:

Email Used solely for authentication. Never shared for marketing purposes.
Username Randomly generated at registration. Visible to other users on comments.
Avatar Stored as a Steam App ID reference. No image data is uploaded.
ℹ️

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, or any other personal identifiers beyond what is listed above.

How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity when you sign in
  • Allow you to recover access to your account

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

Community Content

Comments you post within the app are stored in our database and visible to other users. You may delete your own comments at any time from within the app.

Votes (likes/dislikes) you cast on comments are stored and associated with your account solely to prevent duplicate voting.

Third-Party Services

The app retrieves publicly available game data from the following external services. No personal data is sent to any of them.

Steam API Game details, reviews, player counts — store.steampowered.com
SteamSpy Ownership and playtime estimates — steamspy.com
Steam Web API Current player counts — api.steampowered.com

Requests to these services are made using only public game identifiers (App IDs).

Authentication Provider

Account authentication is handled by Supabase, a third-party backend service. Your email and password are stored securely by Supabase using industry-standard encryption. We do not have access to your password at any point.

You can review Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the address listed in section 10.

Children's Privacy

⚠️

Steam Market Analyzer is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
